# STRATEGIES.md

tradefabe repo doc "STRATEGIES.md" — the pre-registered strategy roster, organized into 8
families by edge source (trend, mean reversion, calendar, defensive/low-vol, carry,
volatility risk premium, information-following, and piggyback constructions on the passive
core). Only one strategy is a real survivor: delta-neutral crypto funding carry
(~12%/yr net on Hyperliquid, 2023-26, market-neutral, +1.3% through a −53% BTC crash).
Everything else tested is DEAD, including two info-following bets (congressional-purchase
copying, insider-buying copying) and — after the v1.3 Bonferroni correction — 3 of 4
piggyback constructions that had looked ALIVE under the uncorrected bar. See [[tradefabe]].
