# ECE 270 — Fields and Waves (spring 2026)

**ECE 270, Fields and Waves** (confirmed by Dave, 2026-07-17) — transmission lines and
electromagnetics, spring 2026.

The `uni/spring 2026/270/notion notes/` folder gives a clean topic sequence: transmission-line
fundamentals → critical lengths → transients/bounce diagrams → AC steady-state & input impedance →
power delivery & phasors → impedance matching → EM plane waves → lossy media → reflection at normal
& oblique incidence, antennas. ^[[uni/]] Also holds homework, labs, practice exams. Vault also has
Notion notes and Drive lab reports (`raw/drive/270/` — wave-motion demonstrator, optics lab; one
lab extracted thinly, flagged).

## Concepts (deep-ingested 2026-07-18)

- [[transmission-line-theory]] — $Z_0$, $v_p$, wave expressions, reflection coefficient, input-impedance master equation
- [[bounce-diagrams-and-transients]] — step response, source/load bounces, time-domain reflectometry
- [[impedance-matching]] — single-stub match (analytical + Smith chart), quarter-wave transformer
- [[electromagnetic-plane-waves]] — E/H fields, intrinsic impedance $\eta$, polarization, power density
- [[wave-reflection-at-boundaries]] — normal-incidence $\Gamma$/$\tau$, $|\Gamma|^2$, AR coatings & radomes
- [[waves-in-lossy-media]] — complex permittivity, loss tangent, attenuation, skin depth
- [[oblique-incidence-and-antennas]] — Brewster angle, Snell's law, dipole antennas, gain, Friis equation
- [[wave-impedance-analogy]] — the unifying idea: TL, EM, acoustic & quantum waves share one reflection law
  (folds in the acoustics and quantum finals as instances)

The [[wave-impedance-analogy]] is the course's through-line and links out to
[[electronic-band-structure]] ([[230-semiconductors]]). **Source error flagged inline** in
[[wave-reflection-at-boundaries]]: the radome example's $v=c/\epsilon_r$ (correct: $c/\sqrt{\epsilon_r}$;
the numeric answer is right) — see [[contradictions]]. Drive lab reports left as source cards.
