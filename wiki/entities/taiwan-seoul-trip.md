# Taiwan → Seoul trip (Aug 2026)

A roughly one-week solo trip through Taiwan that ends by delivering Dave into Seoul to begin his
**fall 2026 Yonsei exchange** — the travel and the study-abroad move are one continuous plan. ^[[sources/drive-personal-taiwan-seoul-itinerary]]

**Shape of the trip:**
- **LAX → Taipei** (Aug 21), a few days in Taipei weighted toward art/design and food — Huashan &
  Songshan creative parks, MOCA, the Ri Xing type foundry, Ningxia/Raohe night markets, Elephant
  Mountain at sunset, Zhongshan bars.
- **HSR south to Kaohsiung** — Pier-2 Art Center, Dome of Light, ferry to Cijin, harbor walks.
- **Beidawu Mountain** — a two-day summit hike (lodge night, ~1:30 AM alpine start for sunrise),
  gated on a mountain-entry permit, lodge booking, and hiking insurance.
- **Kaohsiung → Seoul** (Aug 28), then straight to the Yonsei area to move into housing.

**Study-abroad transition (facts, sensitive details deliberately omitted):** the trip terminates in
the [[academic-timeline|fall 2026 Yonsei exchange]] — D-2 student visa applied, Yonsei on-campus-area
housing (Enkostay) confirmed pending an in-person lease signing, and health-insurance coverage routed
through Duke's SMIP plan. Application numbers, the housing/lease code, contact phone numbers, booking
links, and a site login were in the source doc and are **not** copied here (see the source card).
